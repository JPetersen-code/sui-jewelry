Our Story

Small studio, Sydney-made, inspired by water.

How We Started

Sui — the word for water.

Sui Jewelry began as a simple idea: rings that feel like a moment on the water, made with the quiet, considered approach we love in Japanese design. We're a small team working out of a home workshop in Sydney, learning and refining our craft with every batch.

We're just starting out — right now that means short runs, hands-on finishing, and a lot of care put into each piece before it ever reaches you.

Our Process

From sketch to finished ring.

1. Observe

Every collection starts with a specific water movement — a ripple, a tide line, moonlight on a dark surface — sketched until the form feels right.

2. Shape

Each ring is worked by hand in our Sydney workshop, in small batches, across sterling silver, gold, rose gold and titanium.

3. Finish

Final polishing, texture and quality checks happen one piece at a time before anything is listed for sale.

Where We Are

Based in Sydney, sold worldwide via Etsy.

We currently sell exclusively through our Etsy store, which handles secure checkout, sizing requests and shipping for us. This site exists to tell the fuller story and show the whole collection in one place.
