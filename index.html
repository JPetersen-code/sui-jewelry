Rings shaped like water.

Sui (水) means water. We make small-batch, Japanese-style rings in silver, gold and titanium — each one drawing on waves, ripples, tides and the moon on still water. Designed and made by hand in Sydney.

Our Approach

Minimal forms, drawn from water.

Every piece starts with a simple question: what does this moment on the water look like, in metal? A wave curling over. A droplet about to fall. The moon breaking across a dark tide. We keep the forms quiet and the craftsmanship close — each ring is made in small batches, by hand, in our Sydney workshop.
