Care & FAQ

Looking after your ring — and a few common questions.

Ring Sizing

Finding your size.

Not sure of your ring size? The easiest way is to measure a ring you already own that fits the intended finger well.

  1. Measure the inside diameter of an existing ring, in millimetres.
  2. Compare that against an Australian/international ring size chart — we link to one on each Etsy listing.
  3. If you're between two sizes, we generally recommend sizing up slightly, especially for wider bands.
  4. Still unsure? Message us — we're happy to help before you order.
Metal Care

Caring for each metal.

Metal Everyday wear Cleaning Avoid
Sterling Silver Durable for daily wear; will naturally patina over time. Polish gently with a soft cloth; occasional silver dip for deeper tarnish. Chlorine, prolonged water exposure, household chemicals.
9k / 14k Gold Excellent for daily wear; resists tarnish well. Warm water, mild soap, soft brush; buff dry with a cloth. Chlorine (pools/spas), abrasive cleaners.
Rose Gold Durable; colour may soften very slightly with years of wear. Same as gold — warm water, mild soap, soft cloth. Chlorine, harsh chemicals, abrasive polishing.
Titanium Extremely hard-wearing and scratch-resistant; great for active wear. Wipe with a soft cloth; mild soap and water as needed. Cannot be resized by traditional methods — order carefully.
General rule: take rings off before swimming, showering with heavy product use, exercising, or cleaning with household chemicals.
